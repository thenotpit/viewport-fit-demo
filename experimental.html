TAP A SHAPE

The strips are on every device here and the page sits still on a night sky. Every shape has a color of its own. Tap one, as often as you like: the strips jump to that color and fade back into the sky over a second, with no scrolling involved and no script. The cyan and yellow are too light for white text, so their flashes use the same hue toned down to keep 4.5:1.
On the Mac and iPad the first tap locks the bar, so the flash never reaches it there.
