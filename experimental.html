Viewport Cover Color Strip Hack Variable Strips Experimental Experimental 2

Demo by mrtipton.com

Experimental.

The strips are on every device here and the page sits still on one flat blue. Tap any white shape, as often as you like: the strips jump to white and fade back to blue over a second, with no scrolling involved and no script. If the bars flash, Safari reads a strip whenever it repaints.
On the Mac and iPad the first tap locks the bar, so the flash never reaches it there.
