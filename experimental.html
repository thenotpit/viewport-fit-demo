Viewport Cover Color Strip Hack Variable Strips Experimental Experimental 2

Demo by mrtipton.com

Experimental.

The strips are on every device here and pulse between blue and pink with no scrolling involved, starting at four beats a second, speeding up to sixteen, and slowing back down. The page itself stays blue. If the bars pulse, Safari reads a strip whenever it repaints, not only on scroll.
Tap a white shape: the strips jump to white, fade back to blue, and the pulse starts over. Still no script; the shapes are labels for hidden radio buttons.
On the Mac and iPad, expect the pulse to run until the first scroll and then lock.
