Viewport Cover Color Strip Hack Variable Strips Experimental Experimental 2

Demo by mrtipton.com

Experimental.

The strips are on every device here and the page sits still on one flat blue. Every shape has a color of its own. Tap one, as often as you like: the strips jump to its color and fade back to blue over a second, with no scrolling involved and no script. Every color keeps white text at 4.5:1 or better, so none of them trips the switch to dark text.
On the Mac and iPad the first tap locks the bar, so the flash never reaches it there.
